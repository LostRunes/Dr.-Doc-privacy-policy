1. what dr. doc does with your files

Dr. Doc converts, compresses and edits files entirely on your own device. When you add a file, the app reads it, processes it locally and saves the result to the output folder you choose (by default Downloads\Dr. Doc on Windows, or a Dr. Doc folder on Android). Files are never uploaded to any server, ours or anyone else's.

Files you drag into the window, and intermediate files created during a conversion, are kept temporarily in the app's local cache folder on your PC. You can delete them at any time with Clean up → Clear app cache, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.

2. data we collect

None. Dr. Doc has:

No accountsNo sign-up or sign-in, ever.
No analyticsNo telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
No adsNo advertising, cookies or trackers.
No internetThe app works fully offline. The Android app doesn't even have internet permission.

The app's window talks only to its own built-in engine over a local connection (127.0.0.1) that is not reachable from the internet or your local network. Your settings (for example theme, output folder and last-used options) are stored only on your PC.

3. android: camera, photos & videos

The Android app asks for these permissions only when you use the feature that needs them, and everything stays on your phone:

  • Camera: to take pictures of pages in Scan → PDF. Photos are turned into a PDF on the phone and are never sent anywhere.
  • Photos and videos: so Clean up can look through your gallery for duplicate and very large files, and so you can pick pictures to convert. The app only reads them to show you results; nothing is uploaded, and nothing is deleted unless you confirm it.

Dr. Doc for Android does not request internet access, so it is technically unable to send your files or any data off your phone. You can revoke any permission at any time in Android's settings.

4. microsoft office integration (windows)

If Microsoft Word, Excel or PowerPoint is installed, Dr. Doc can use them on your PC to convert Office documents. This happens locally. Dr. Doc sends nothing to Microsoft. Your use of Office itself is governed by Microsoft's privacy statement.

5. microsoft store & google play

Microsoft and Google may collect data about app downloads, installs and crashes as part of the Microsoft Store, Windows, Google Play and Android. That is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement and Google's Privacy Policy, not by Dr. Doc.

6. children

Dr. Doc collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.
